Use these checklists to structure an internal review. Your exact requirements will depend on your environment and business.

01BACKUP & RECOVERY

Could you restore what the business needs?

A backup job that reports success is only part of the picture. Review coverage, ownership and restore readiness together.

  • List the important data, systems and services that need protection.
  • Confirm backup coverage, retention and who checks for failures.
  • Check where copies are kept and who can access or change them.
  • Run a restore test and record the steps, result and time required.
  • Write down recovery priorities and who coordinates the response.
Good next stepChoose one important system and schedule a test restore.
02MICROSOFT 365 & IDENTITY

Are accounts and access still appropriate?

Small changes in roles and people can leave access untidy. A regular review helps keep permissions aligned with how the team works.

  • Confirm multi-factor authentication is enabled for users and administrators.
  • Review administrator roles and remove access that is no longer needed.
  • Check that leavers and dormant accounts are disabled appropriately.
  • Review guest users, external sharing and mailbox forwarding rules.
  • Make sure the process for urgent or emergency access is documented.
Good next stepReview administrator accounts and confirm each one has a current owner.
03SERVERS & NETWORK

Do you know what your core systems depend on?

A current, lightweight inventory makes support, maintenance and future changes easier to plan.

  • Record server, network and cloud services, their purpose and owner.
  • Note operating system versions, support dates and maintenance windows.
  • Map key dependencies, such as identity, DNS, storage and connectivity.
  • Check patching, capacity alerts, remote access and backup status.
  • Capture known risks, documentation gaps and a review date.
Good next stepStart with a simple list of systems and the business services they support.

These checklists are general prompts, not a security audit or a substitute for advice tailored to your systems and obligations.
